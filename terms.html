How You Join

You join by checking the consent box on a form at vitaltouchcares.com and giving us your mobile number, or by texting or calling us first. We never add a number that was not given to us directly, and we do not buy, rent, or import contact lists.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase or of receiving any service from us.

Messages you may receive include:

  • Replies to an enquiry you sent us
  • Scheduling, confirming, or changing a visit or appointment
  • Updates about care we are providing
  • Application and interview updates, if you applied to work with us

Frequency, Cost, and Support

  • Message frequency varies depending on your enquiry and the care being scheduled.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Those charges come from your mobile carrier, not from us.
