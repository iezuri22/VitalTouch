Medicare Advantage

Many plans include some help at home each year. Most people never hear about it.

Tell us your plan. We’ll find out what it covers and call you.

Original Medicare only

It doesn’t pay for everyday help at home. Another option on this list usually does.

Tell us below and we’ll find which one fits.

Medicaid

It can pay for help at home if you qualify. We can’t bill Medicaid yet.

Tell us below. We’ll tell you if you likely qualify and who can help.

VA benefits

Veterans and surviving spouses can often get help at home through the VA.

Tell us below and we’ll tell you who to ask.

Long-term care insurance

Most policies pay for care at home, not just nursing homes.

Tell us the company. We’ll check the policy with you and do the paperwork.

Under 60 with a disability

The Illinois Home Services Program may help. We aren’t approved for it yet.

Tell us below. We’ll point you to the right office.

Paying on your own

Month to month. No minimums. Start small and add hours when you need them.
