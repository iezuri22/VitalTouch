Find your route

Each plan, policy and program has its own rules. Here is how each one works.

On income

Medicaid

Covers help at home for people who qualify on income and need, usually at no cost. A care assessment sets the hours.

We can’t bill Medicaid yet. We’ll tell you if you likely qualify, and point you to an agency that can bill it if you need care sooner.

Call us and we’ll tell you where you likely stand.

Under 60

Home Services Program

Illinois funds help at home for adults under 60 living with a disability. It has its own rules and application, and often works alongside Social Security disability benefits.

We aren’t approved for this program yet. We’re working on it. Apply anyway if it fits, because approval takes time.

Call and ask. We’ll point you to the right office, whether or not you use us.

By plan

Medicare Advantage extras

Many plans include in-home support hours each year. It’s rarely mentioned at enrollment.

  • Look for in-home support, personal care, homemaker or caregiver support
  • Some plans call it a flex allowance or benefit card
  • Hours usually reset each plan year, and unused hours are lost
  • Benefits vary by plan, even from one insurer

Tell us the plan name and we’ll find out what it includes.

Veterans

VA benefits

Homemaker and Home Health Aide pays an agency for everyday help. Veteran-Directed Care gives a budget to choose a caregiver. Aid and Attendance adds to a VA pension.

  • Ask a VA social worker for a Homemaker and Home Health Aide referral
  • Surviving spouses may qualify for Aid and Attendance
  • Separate from Medicare and Medicaid
  • No one should charge you to file a VA claim

Ask the VA social worker, or call us to find who to ask.

Policy

Long-term care insurance

Most policies cover care at home, not just nursing homes. Many families never file because the paperwork looks hard.

  • The daily or monthly benefit (the most it pays)
  • The elimination period (waiting days)
  • Whether it requires a licensed agency (we are one)
  • Whether it needs an assessment first

Bring the policy to a free consultation. We’ll read it with you and handle the visit records the insurer asks for.

Everyone

Paying on your own

The most flexible option. It also covers the gap while a program application is pending.

  • No minimums, month to month
  • Start small, add hours as needed
  • Monthly plans for a set schedule and price

See monthly care plans or our rates, then call for a quote.
