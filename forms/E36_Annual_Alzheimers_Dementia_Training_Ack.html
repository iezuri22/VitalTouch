ANNUAL ALZHEIMER'S AND DEMENTIA TRAINING — VitalTouch Health Network Inc.

In accordance with the Illinois Alzheimer's Disease Assistance Act and IDPH regulations, all employees who provide direct care to clients with Alzheimer's disease or related dementias must receive specialized training.

This training covers the following topics:

1. Understanding Alzheimer's Disease and Dementia

  • Types of dementia (Alzheimer's, vascular, Lewy body, frontotemporal)
  • Stages and progression of Alzheimer's disease
  • Current research and treatments

2. Communication Strategies

  • Communicating with individuals with cognitive impairment
  • Non-verbal communication techniques
  • Active listening and patience
  • Reducing confusion and agitation through communication

3. Behavioral Management

  • Understanding behavioral changes in dementia
  • Common behaviors: wandering, sundowning, aggression, repetition
  • Non-pharmacological interventions
  • Creating a calm and supportive environment

4. Safety Considerations

  • Home safety modifications for dementia clients
  • Wandering prevention and response
  • Fall prevention strategies
  • Medication safety

5. Activities of Daily Living (ADLs)

  • Assisting with personal care while maintaining dignity
  • Nutrition and hydration challenges
  • Sleep disturbances and management
  • Maintaining routines and structure

6. Caregiver Self-Care

  • Recognizing caregiver stress and burnout
  • Resources for support
  • Setting healthy boundaries
  • When to seek help

7. Legal and Ethical Considerations

  • Decision-making capacity
  • Advance directives and guardianship
  • Reporting abuse and neglect
  • Maintaining dignity and respect

This training satisfies the annual dementia care training requirement. Records are maintained per IDPH regulations.
