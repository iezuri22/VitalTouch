Terms & Conditions
Please review the terms of this service agreement.

1. Services

VitalTouch Health Network Inc. ("Agency") agrees to provide non-medical home services to the Client as authorized by the Client's Plan of Care and approved by the applicable payer source. Services will be performed by trained homemakers or certified nursing assistants employed by the Agency.

2. Client Rights

The Client has the right to:

  • Receive services in a safe, respectful, and dignified manner
  • Participate in the development and revision of their Plan of Care
  • Be informed of any changes to services, schedule, or assigned caregiver
  • Refuse any service or caregiver assignment
  • File a complaint without fear of retaliation
  • Access their records upon written request
  • Privacy and confidentiality of personal and health information (HIPAA)
  • Be free from abuse, neglect, and exploitation

3. Client Responsibilities

The Client agrees to:

  • Maintain a safe and accessible home environment for the caregiver
  • Provide reasonable notice (at least 24 hours) when canceling a scheduled visit
  • Notify the Agency of any changes in health condition, medication, or emergency contacts
  • Treat caregivers with respect and dignity
  • Not request caregivers to perform tasks outside the scope of their authorized services

4. Agency Responsibilities

VitalTouch Health Network agrees to:

  • Provide qualified, trained, and background-checked caregivers
  • Supervise caregivers in accordance with IDPH regulations (77 Ill. Adm. Code Part 245)
  • Conduct an initial supervisory visit within 90 days and ongoing visits per regulation
  • Maintain all required insurance and licensure
  • Respond to client concerns within 24 business hours
  • Replace a caregiver upon reasonable request

5. Billing & Payment

Services will be billed to the payer source identified in this agreement. If you are paying for your own care, the current hourly rate is given to you in writing and any changes will require 30 days advance notice. The Client is responsible for any services not covered by their payer source.

6. Cancellation & Discharge

Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice. The Agency may discharge a client immediately if the home environment poses a safety risk to the caregiver, or if the Client engages in abusive or threatening behavior. The Agency will assist with transition to another provider when possible.

7. Complaints & Grievances

Complaints may be directed to VitalTouch Health Network at (708) 774-4850 or info@vitaltouchcares.com. Complaints may also be filed with the Illinois Department of Public Health at 1-800-252-4343.

8. Confidentiality

All client information is protected under HIPAA and Illinois state law. The Agency will not disclose personal or health information without written consent except as required by law or for treatment, payment, and healthcare operations.
