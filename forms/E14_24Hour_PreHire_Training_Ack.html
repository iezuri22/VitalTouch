24-HOUR PRE-HIRE TRAINING

Required under 77 Ill. Adm. Code Part 245

As required by the Illinois Department of Public Health for Home Services Agencies, all homemakers must complete a minimum of 24 hours of training prior to providing services. This training covers the following topics:

1. Overview of Home Services Agency Regulations

  • IDPH licensing requirements
  • Role and responsibilities of homemakers

2. Client Rights and Responsibilities

  • Right to privacy and dignity
  • Right to participate in care planning
  • Right to refuse services

3. Communication Skills

  • Effective communication with clients
  • Cultural sensitivity and diversity
  • Working with clients with cognitive impairments

4. Personal Care Assistance

  • Activities of Daily Living (ADLs)
  • Instrumental Activities of Daily Living (IADLs)
  • Assisting with mobility and transfers

5. Safety and Emergency Procedures

  • Home safety assessment
  • Fall prevention
  • Emergency response protocols
  • Fire safety

6. Infection Control

  • Hand hygiene
  • Universal precautions
  • Bloodborne pathogen awareness

7. Nutrition and Meal Preparation

  • Basic nutrition for elderly and disabled clients
  • Special dietary needs
  • Food safety and handling

8. Housekeeping Standards

  • Cleaning procedures
  • Laundry protocols
  • Maintaining a safe home environment

9. Documentation and Reporting

  • Service documentation requirements
  • Electronic Visit Verification (EVV)
  • Incident reporting

10. Abuse, Neglect, and Exploitation Prevention

  • Recognizing signs of abuse/neglect
  • Mandatory reporting requirements
  • Reporting procedures

11. HIPAA and Confidentiality

  • Protected Health Information (PHI)
  • Privacy practices
  • Social media policy

12. Professional Conduct

  • Boundaries with clients
  • Attendance and punctuality
  • Dress code and personal hygiene
