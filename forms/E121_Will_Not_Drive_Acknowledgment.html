TRANSPORTATION POLICY ACKNOWLEDGMENT — VitalTouch Health Network Inc.

As an employee of VitalTouch Health Network Inc., I understand and acknowledge the following regarding transportation:

  1. I will NOT be required to drive clients in my personal vehicle or any vehicle as part of my job duties.
  2. I will NOT transport clients to medical appointments, errands, or any other destinations.
  3. If a client requests transportation, I will decline and notify my supervisor immediately.
  4. VitalTouch Health Network does not provide auto insurance coverage for employees' personal vehicles.
  5. Any transportation of clients is strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
  6. If I observe any safety concerns related to a client's transportation needs, I will report them to my supervisor.

This policy is in place to protect both employees and clients. If you have questions about this policy, please contact your supervisor or the VitalTouch office at (708) 774-4850.
