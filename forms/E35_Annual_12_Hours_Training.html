ANNUAL 12-HOUR TRAINING REQUIREMENT — VitalTouch Health Network Inc.

As required by the Illinois Department of Public Health (77 Ill. Adm. Code Part 245), all homemakers employed by a licensed Home Services Agency must complete a minimum of 12 hours of continuing education/training annually.

Training Topics (minimum 12 hours total):

  1. MandatoryClient Rights and Responsibilities
  2. MandatoryAbuse, Neglect, and Exploitation Prevention
  3. RecommendedInfection Control and Universal Precautions
  4. RecommendedEmergency Procedures and Safety
  5. RecommendedHIPAA and Confidentiality Review
  6. ElectiveCultural Sensitivity and Diversity
  7. ElectiveCommunication Skills
  8. ElectiveDocumentation and Reporting
  9. ElectivePersonal Care Techniques
  10. ElectiveNutrition and Meal Preparation
  11. RecommendedAlzheimer's/Dementia Care (if serving dementia clients)
  12. ElectiveAny additional training as determined by VitalTouch management

Training may be completed through:

  • In-person classroom sessions
  • Online/video training modules
  • Supervised on-the-job training
  • External conferences or workshops (with prior approval)

All training must be documented and records maintained by VitalTouch Health Network as required by IDPH regulations. Failure to complete the annual 12-hour training requirement may result in the employee being unable to continue providing services until the requirement is met.
