NOTICE OF PRIVACY PRACTICES — VitalTouch Health Network Inc.
▼

1. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

VitalTouch Health Network Inc. is committed to protecting the privacy and security of all Protected Health Information (PHI) in accordance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and the Illinois Personal Information Protection Act.

2. What is Protected Health Information (PHI)?

PHI includes any individually identifiable health information, whether oral, written, or electronic, that relates to:

  • A client's past, present, or future physical or mental health condition
  • The provision of healthcare services to a client
  • Past, present, or future payment for healthcare services
  • Any information that can be used to identify a specific client (name, address, date of birth, Social Security number, etc.)
PC
3. Your Responsibilities as an Employee
▼

As an employee of VitalTouch Health Network, you are required to:

  • Access only the minimum necessary PHI required to perform your job duties
  • Never share client information with unauthorized individuals, including family members, friends, or other clients
  • Never discuss client information in public areas where others may overhear
  • Properly secure all paper and electronic records containing PHI
  • Report any suspected breach of PHI to your supervisor immediately
  • Never post client information on social media or any public platform
  • Return all documents containing PHI upon termination of employment
PC
4. Permitted Uses and Disclosures
▼

VitalTouch may use and disclose PHI for:

  • Treatment — coordinating care with other healthcare providers
  • Payment — billing insurance companies or Medicaid
  • Healthcare Operations — quality assessment, training, and compliance activities
  • As Required by Law — mandatory reporting, court orders, or public health activities

5. Client Rights

Clients have the right to:

  • Request access to their PHI
  • Request amendments to their records
  • Request restrictions on certain uses/disclosures
  • Request confidential communications
  • Receive an accounting of disclosures
  • File a complaint with VitalTouch or the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services
BR
6. Reporting Breaches
▼

If you become aware of any breach or suspected breach of PHI, you must report it immediately to:

  • Your direct supervisor
  • Ifeanyi Ezurike, CEO/Privacy Officer — (708) 774-4850 — iezuri22@gmail.com

Failure to report a known breach is itself a HIPAA violation.

PE
7. Consequences of HIPAA Violations
▼

Violations of HIPAA regulations may result in:

  • Disciplinary action, up to and including immediate termination
  • Civil penalties ranging from $100 to $50,000 per violation
  • Criminal penalties including fines up to $250,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years
  • Personal liability for damages resulting from unauthorized disclosures
