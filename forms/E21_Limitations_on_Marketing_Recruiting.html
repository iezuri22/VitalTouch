Marketing & Recruiting Limitations Policy
⚠️ This policy applies to all employees and independent contractors of VitalTouch Health Network Inc. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
MR

Marketing Restrictions

Sections 1-2: Unauthorized Marketing & Client Solicitation

❯

1. Prohibition on Unauthorized Marketing

I shall not engage in any marketing, advertising, or promotional activities on behalf of the Agency without prior written authorization from the CEO or designated supervisor. This includes but is not limited to:

  • Distributing flyers, brochures, or business cards
  • Posting on social media on behalf of the Agency
  • Making representations about the Agency's services to potential clients or referral sources
  • Advertising the Agency's services in any medium

2. Prohibition on Client Solicitation

I shall not directly or indirectly solicit clients for the Agency or for any competing home services provider. I will not:

  • Approach individuals at hospitals, nursing facilities, or community centers to solicit business
  • Offer incentives, gifts, or payments to potential clients or their family members in exchange for enrolling in services
  • Make promises regarding services, schedules, or benefits that have not been authorized by the Agency
RC

Recruiting Conduct

Sections 3-7: Non-Solicitation, Compliance & Consequences

❯

3. Non-Solicitation of Agency Staff

During my employment and for a period of twelve (12) months following separation, I will not recruit, solicit, or encourage any Agency employee to leave VitalTouch Health Network to work for a competitor or for my own benefit.

4. Non-Solicitation of Agency Clients

During my employment and for a period of twelve (12) months following separation, I will not solicit or attempt to redirect any current or former client of VitalTouch Health Network to another provider or to receive services privately outside of the Agency.

5. Compliance with Illinois Regulations

I understand that all marketing activities must comply with the Illinois Home Health, Home Services, and Home Nursing Agency Licensing Act (210 ILCS 55) and the Illinois Administrative Code (77 Ill. Adm. Code Part 245). Any marketing materials must be truthful, not misleading, and accurately represent the services provided by the Agency.

6. Reporting Obligations

If I become aware of any unauthorized marketing or recruiting activities by any employee, contractor, or third party acting on behalf of the Agency, I am required to report such activities to my supervisor or the CEO immediately.

7. Consequences of Violation

Violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including immediate termination of employment, and may also result in legal action to enforce non-solicitation provisions and recover damages.
