CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Consulting Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between VitalTouch Health Network Inc. ("Company"), located at 18000 Dixie Hwy, Homewood, IL 60430, and the Consultant identified in Step 1 of this form.

1. Independent Contractor Status

The Consultant is engaged as an independent contractor and not as an employee. The Consultant shall not be entitled to any employee benefits, including but not limited to health insurance, retirement plans, workers' compensation, or paid time off. The Consultant is responsible for their own taxes, including self-employment tax, and will receive an IRS Form 1099 for compensation paid.

2. Scope of Services

The Consultant agrees to perform the services described in this Agreement in a professional and workmanlike manner. The specific scope of services, deliverables, and timelines shall be as described in the engagement details provided by the Consultant. Any changes to the scope must be agreed upon in writing by both parties.

3. Compensation & Expenses

The Company shall pay the Consultant at the rate specified in this Agreement. The Consultant shall submit invoices detailing services performed and hours worked (if applicable). Reasonable, pre-approved expenses will be reimbursed upon submission of receipts. Payment will be made according to the selected payment terms.

4. Confidentiality

The Consultant acknowledges that during the engagement they may have access to confidential and proprietary information of the Company, including but not limited to client lists, business strategies, financial data, employee information, and protected health information (PHI). The Consultant agrees to:

  • Keep all confidential information strictly confidential during and after the engagement
  • Not disclose confidential information to any third party without prior written consent
  • Comply with HIPAA regulations if access to PHI is required
  • Return all Company materials and data upon termination of this Agreement

5. Non-Solicitation

During the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months following its termination, the Consultant shall not directly or indirectly solicit, recruit, or encourage any employee, contractor, or client of VitalTouch Health Network to terminate their relationship with the Company.

6. Intellectual Property

All work product, materials, documents, and deliverables created by the Consultant in the course of performing services under this Agreement shall be the sole and exclusive property of VitalTouch Health Network Inc. The Consultant hereby assigns all rights, title, and interest in such work product to the Company.

7. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with fourteen (14) days written notice. The Company may terminate immediately for cause, including breach of confidentiality, failure to perform services, or misconduct. Upon termination, the Consultant shall be compensated for services satisfactorily performed up to the date of termination.

8. Indemnification

The Consultant agrees to indemnify and hold harmless VitalTouch Health Network Inc., its officers, directors, and employees from any claims, damages, or liabilities arising from the Consultant's performance of services under this Agreement or breach of any terms herein.

9. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois. Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall be resolved in the courts of Cook County, Illinois.
