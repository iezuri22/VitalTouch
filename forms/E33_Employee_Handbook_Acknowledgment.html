I acknowledge that I have received a copy of the VitalTouch Health Network Employee Handbook. I understand that:

1. Employment Relationship

My employment with VitalTouch Health Network is at-will. This means that either I or VitalTouch may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice.

2. Handbook Contents

The Employee Handbook contains important information about VitalTouch's policies, procedures, and expectations, including but not limited to:

  • Attendance and punctuality requirements
  • Dress code and personal appearance standards
  • Cell phone and social media policies
  • Workplace conduct expectations
  • Anti-harassment and anti-discrimination policies
  • Safety and emergency procedures
  • Disciplinary procedures

3. Policy Changes

VitalTouch Health Network reserves the right to modify, revise, or eliminate any of the policies described in the handbook at any time. I understand that I will be notified of any significant changes.
