What we do

A few hours a week to daily visits.

Personal care

Help with the most personal parts of the day.

  • Bathing, showering and grooming
  • Dressing and getting ready
  • Toileting and continence care
  • Safe transfers from bed, chair and shower
Get care started

Memory care

Consistent support for people living with dementia.

  • The same caregivers, so faces stay familiar
  • Routines that reduce confusion
  • Gentle redirection and supervision
  • Updates for the family after visits
Get care started

Meals and home

A clean, safe home and good meals.

  • Meal planning and cooking
  • Light housekeeping and laundry
  • Grocery shopping
  • Medication reminders
Get care started

Rides and errands

Appointments, errands and outings, with someone who waits.

  • Doctor and therapy appointments
  • Work, school and errands
  • Pharmacy and store runs
  • Visits with friends and family
Get care started

Companionship

Someone to talk to, walk with and keep an eye on things.

  • Conversation and activities
  • Walks and light exercise
  • Hobbies, games and outings
  • A check-in on mood and appetite
Get care started

Respite for family

Time off for the family caregiver.

  • A few hours, overnight or recurring
  • Same routine, kept the same way
  • Short notice when we can
  • Written notes so nothing is missed
