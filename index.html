Who We Help

Not everyone who needs care is 85

Home care is usually written as though they are. Plenty of the people we support are 35, or 52 — often arranging it for themselves. Pick whichever sounds more like you.

For an aging parent or spouse

Help with bathing and dressing, meals and housekeeping, medication reminders, rides, memory care, and company — as much or as little as the week needs.

For adults living with a disability

Support at any age, directed by you — safe transfers, keeping the household running, and rides to work, school, or appointments. Illinois funds this for adults under 60, through a program most people are never told about.

A Note From Our Founder

This isn’t a franchise. It’s personal.

I started VitalTouch here in the south suburbs because I watched families struggle to find home care they could actually trust — big-name agencies where you never talk to the same person twice, and nobody knows your mother’s name.

We do it differently. We’re licensed by the State of Illinois, our caregivers are trained and background-checked, and when you call, you get a person — often me. Your family’s care is my name on the door.
