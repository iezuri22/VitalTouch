Who we help

Not everyone who needs care is 85

Home care is usually written as though they are. Plenty of the people we support are 35, or 52, and often arranging it for themselves.

For an aging parent or spouse

Bathing and dressing, meals and housekeeping, medication reminders, rides, memory care and company. As much or as little as the week needs.

See our services

For adults living with a disability

Support at any age, directed by you: safe transfers, keeping the household running, and rides to work, school or appointments. Illinois funds this for adults under 60.
