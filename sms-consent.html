Text Messages from VitalTouch Health Network

VitalTouch Health Network, Inc. is a licensed home care agency serving seniors and adults living with disabilities across the Chicagoland area. We provide companionship, personal care and homemaking in the home. This page explains our text message program, and lets you join it.

What we send

  • Replies to an enquiry you sent us about care
  • Scheduling, confirming or changing a visit
  • Updates about care already in progress
  • Application and interview updates, if you applied for a caregiver role

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time, START to resume, or HELP for help. You can also call us at (708) 726-4536 or email ifeanyi@vitaltouch.care.

We never share your mobile number. Mobile information and SMS opt-in data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
