Support for adults living with a disability

In-home help for adults of any age, not only seniors. You decide what help you want and when.

How we work

  • You direct the care. You decide what gets done, how, and when.
  • Only the help you ask for. Needing help with one task doesn't mean you need help with everything.
  • The same caregiver. We schedule for continuity, so you aren't retraining someone new each week.
  • Easy to switch. If a match isn't working, tell us and we'll change it.
  • Trained caregivers. Our caregivers are trained in disability awareness.

What we help with

Personal care and transfers

Bathing, dressing, grooming, and safe help in and out of bed, a chair, or the shower.

Household

Meals, laundry, linens, light housekeeping, groceries, and errands.

Rides

To work, school, dialysis, appointments, or anywhere else.

Company

Conversation and a second pair of hands, as much or as little as you want.

Communication support

Patience with speech, hearing, or vision needs. We talk to you, not about you.

Respite for family

Scheduled breaks for the family member who usually provides care.

Who we support

No diagnosis list required. People come to us living with MS, cerebral palsy, spinal cord and brain injuries, stroke, Parkinson's, ALS, developmental disabilities, chronic illness, and recovery from major surgery.

What we don't do

We are non-medical. No wound care, injections, catheter changes, or ventilator management. If you need nursing, we'll tell you on the first call and point you to who does it. Many people use a nurse for that and us for everything else.

Paying for it

Illinois funds in-home support for adults under 60 living with a disability, through a program separate from senior programs. Other routes: Medicaid, Medicare Advantage extra benefits, VA benefits, and long-term care insurance.
