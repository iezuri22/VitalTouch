What we help with

Only the help you ask for.

Personal care and transfers

Bathing, dressing, grooming, and safe help in and out of bed, a chair, or the shower.

Household

Meals, laundry, linens, light housekeeping, groceries, and errands.

Rides

To work, school, dialysis, appointments, or anywhere else.

Company

Conversation and a second pair of hands, as much or as little as you want.

Communication support

Patience with speech, hearing, or vision needs. We talk to you, not about you.

Family

Respite for family

Scheduled breaks for the family member who usually provides care.

How we work

Your home, your routine, your call.

  • You direct the careYou decide what gets done, how, and when.
  • Only the help you ask forHelp with one task doesn't mean help with everything.
  • The same caregiverWe schedule for continuity, so you aren't retraining someone new each week.
  • Easy to switchIf a match isn't working, tell us and we'll change it.
  • Trained caregiversOur caregivers are trained in disability awareness.

Paying for it

Illinois funds in-home support for adults under 60 living with a disability.

Under 60

Illinois Home Services Program

A state program for adults under 60, separate from senior programs. We aren’t approved for it yet; we’ll help you apply either way.
