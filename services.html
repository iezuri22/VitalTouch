What we do

Pick what you need. We build the plan around it.

HomemakingA clean, safe home and meals handled.
  • Light housekeeping
  • Laundry and linens
  • Meal planning and cooking
  • Groceries and errands
CompanionshipCompany and conversation, so no one is alone all day.
  • Conversation and visits
  • Games and puzzles
  • Walks and light exercise
  • A check-in on mood
ErrandsWe pick things up so you don’t have to go out.
  • Groceries
  • Medication from the pharmacy
  • Household errands
Personal careHands-on help from Certified Nursing Assistants.
  • Bathing and dressing
  • Toileting and continence care
  • Transfers and mobility
  • Medication reminders
Dementia careNon-medical support from caregivers trained in dementia care.
  • Daily routines
  • Calming and redirection
  • Safety supervision
  • Wandering prevention
Family caregiversAlready have someone you trust? They can be the caregiver.

If they qualify, we hire them, train them and pay them to care for your loved one. You get a caregiver who already knows the family, with our training, care plan and backup behind them.

Some programs have their own rules, for example about spouses. Tell us who you have in mind and we’ll check.

RespiteTime off for the family member doing it all.
  • A few hours or overnight
  • One-time or recurring
  • Same routine, kept the same way
  • Notes after every visit
