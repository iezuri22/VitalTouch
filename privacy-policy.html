Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last Updated: March 3, 2026
VitalTouch Health Network Inc., doing business as VitalTouch Health Network ("we," "us," or "our"), is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website vitaltouchcares.com, use our services, or communicate with us.
1

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, mailing address
  • Care Recipient Information: Name, age, health conditions, care needs, and preferences
  • Employment Application Information: Resume, work history, certifications, background check consent
  • Insurance and Payment Information: Medicaid ID, insurance details, billing information
  • Emergency Contact Information: Names and phone numbers of emergency contacts

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Device information and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring website addresses
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies
2

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our home care services
  • Process inquiries and respond to your requests
  • Schedule and coordinate care services
  • Process employment applications and verify qualifications
  • Communicate with you about appointments, services, and updates
  • Send appointment reminders and care-related notifications
  • Process billing and insurance claims
  • Comply with legal obligations and regulatory requirements
  • Improve our website and user experience
  • Protect against fraud and unauthorized access
3

SMS/Text Message Communications

Text Message Consent

By providing your phone number and opting in to receive text messages from VitalTouch Health Network, you consent to receive SMS communications for service-related purposes.

Text messages we may send include:

  • Appointment reminders and confirmations
  • Care schedule updates
  • Important service notifications
  • Application status updates (for job applicants)
  • Verification codes for account access

Opting Out of Text Messages

You may opt out of receiving text messages at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any text message you receive from us
  • Contacting us at (708) 898-8831
  • Emailing us at ifeanyi@vitaltouch.care

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on your interaction with our services.

4

How We Share Your Information

We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share your information with:

  • Caregivers and Staff: To provide care services to you or your loved one
  • Healthcare Providers: When necessary for care coordination (with your consent)
  • Insurance Companies and Medicaid: To process claims and verify coverage
  • Service Providers: Third parties who assist us in operating our website and business
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • Safety: To protect the rights, safety, and property of VitalTouch, our clients, or others
5

HIPAA Compliance

As a home care provider, we are committed to protecting your health information in accordance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). We maintain appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect the privacy and security of your protected health information (PHI).

Your Rights Regarding Health Information

  • Access and obtain copies of your health records
  • Request corrections to your health information
  • Request restrictions on certain uses and disclosures
  • Receive a list of disclosures we have made
  • Receive a copy of our Notice of Privacy Practices
6

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Encryption of sensitive data in transit and at rest
  • Secure access controls and authentication
  • Regular security assessments and updates
  • Employee training on data protection practices
  • Physical security measures for paper records

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Understand how you use our website
  • Improve our services

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect your ability to use certain features of our website.

8

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

9

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 18, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

10

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your information (subject to legal requirements)
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of marketing communications

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

11

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services to you
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements

Care records and employment records are retained in accordance with Illinois state law and healthcare regulations.

12

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on our website and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of our services after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
